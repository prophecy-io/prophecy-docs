---
title: Migrate to BigQuery SQL
description: How to migrate from Informatica to BigQuery SQL
---

When you create an Informatica bundle for BigQuery SQL in the Prophecy transpiler, you can analyze, change, and validate your migration before the transpilation begins.

## Prerequisite

Export your Informatica workflow.

To export your Informatica workflow from Informatica PowerCenter Designer:

1. Navigate to the workflow you want to export.

1. On the top navigation bar, click **Repository**.

1. Click **Export Objects**.

1. Navigate to where you want to save your file, then click **Save**.

## Limitations

- Prophecy only supports transpiling files from Informatica PowerCenter Designer version 10.4.1 and later.

- Prophecy does not support transpiling your Informatica schedules.

You must [manually configure your schedule](https://docs.prophecy.io/analysts/scheduling) in your Prophecy project after transpiling your Informatica workflow. Then, you can monitor your schedule on [the Monitoring page](https://docs.prophecy.io/analysts/monitoring/#scheduled-pipelines).

## Migration

### Upload your Informatica bundle

1. In the left sidebar, click the **Import** icon (the arrow pointing down in a circle).

1. In the page that opens, click **Import Bundle**.

1. In the **Select source type** page, select **Informatica Powercenter** and click **Next**.

1. In the **Bundle details** page, enter the following information:

   | Field              | Description                                                                                                                                   |
   | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | Import Bundle Name | Name of your bundle.                                                                                                                          |
   | Language           | Click **Change** > **SQL** > **Done**                                                                                                         |
   | Team               | Create or choose the team who will access the bundle.                                                                                         |
   | Project            | Create or choose the [project](https://docs.prophecy.io/projects) for the bundle. <br/>Prophecy filters this list by projects written in SQL. |
   | Branch             | Select the Git branch where you want to upload your code.                                                                                     |
   | SQL Fabric         | Choose the SQL [fabric](https://docs.prophecy.io/fabrics/) to attach your datasets and schedules to.                                          |

1. Click **Next**.

1. Upload the Informatica files you want to convert to SQL pipelines.

   Prophecy accepts the `.xml` file type, which contains structured information about your Informatica workflow.

1. Click **Next**.

### Import pipeline

Next, configure how Prophecy imports your pipelines:

1. Click each file to edit its configurations.

   In the left sidebar, Prophecy lists the files you uploaded in the previous section, which are automatically staged to import. If you upload additional Informatica files on this page, you have to manually stage them to import. All unstaged files can be viewed in the **Pending** tab of the left sidebar.

1. Click **Validate**.

   You must validate your pipeline before you can start transpilation. When you click **Validate**, Prophecy confirms that files to be imported are valid.

1. Click **Transpile**.

### Import transpiled entities

To import the transpiled entities:

1. Click **Import**.

   By default, Prophecy selects all entities for import. To import only a subset of entities, deselect entities.

   ![ImportTranspiledEntities](/import-tool/informatica/informatica-to-sql/img/informatica-import-transpiled-entities.png)

1. Click **Import** in the **Choose entities to create** dialog.

1. In the dialog that opens, click **View Bundle Details**.

### Review Prophecy project

If there isn't an equivalent gem or function for a transformation, Prophecy inserts a placeholder [**ToDo** gem](/data-analysis/gems/custom/todo-gem) in your pipeline. Later, you can write a script or SQL statement to achieve required functionality.

### Review bundle metadata

After you successfully import a bundle, you see the **Imported** status on the Transpiler page.

You can open imported pipelines by hovering over them and clicking the arrow in the far-right column.

You can review the pipelines, jobs, tables, function, coverage, status, source, language, project, and fabric of the bundle in the right tile. You can open any entity in a new tab in the project editor by clicking on the entity.

### Testing

Open the pipeline to verify that it writes the same output as your Informatica workflow. If not, modify or add additional gems to make the output the same.

Before you deploy your project into production, we recommend you run both your Informatica workflow and Prophecy pipeline in parallel for at least two weeks. This helps test if Prophecy consistently produces the same output as Informatica.

### Reimport pipelines

If you want to update or restart your transpilation, click the **Reimport** button at the top right corner of the bundle's page.
