At Prophecy, we are committed to providing you with the support you need, whether you are exploring our platform during a trial or are one of our valued customers.

## Trial users

If you are using a trial version:

- **Reach out on Slack**

  If you have questions about your trial, [join #support on Slack](https://prophecy-io-support.slack.com/archives/C01P1PD7JJY).

  If you have trouble joining, please reach out to `Sales@Prophecy.io` for assistance.

- **Request a Live Demo**

  To see Prophecy in action, [request a live demo of a migration](https://www.prophecy.io/request-a-demo) with our team.

## Existing users

If you are an existing customer:

- **File a Ticket in Our Support Portal**

  Create a free account on our [Customer Support Portal](https://prophecy.zendesk.com/) to track your submitted tickets and receive personalized assistance.

  If you have trouble accessing the Customer Support Portal, please reach out to `Support@Prophecy.io` for assistance.
